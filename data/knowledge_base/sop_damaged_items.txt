**SOP-QC-003: Damaged Items Handling**

**Purpose:** Establish procedures for identifying and handling damaged, defective, or expired products during order fulfilment.

**Examples of Damaged Items**
- Broken or tampered packaging seals.
- Leaking or spilled products.
- Dented or physically damaged packaging.
- Expired or visibly spoiled products.

**Procedure**
1. Inspect the product and identify the type of damage.
2. Never pack food or other products with compromised packaging, leakage, or signs of spoilage.
3. Mark the affected item as damaged in the inventory system.
4. Remove the damaged item from available picking stock and place it in the designated damaged-goods area.
5. Record the SKU, quantity, damage type, and date/time.
6. Arrange a replacement if available; otherwise, mark the item as unavailable or missing in the order.

**Escalation**
- If three or more units of the same SKU are damaged, report the issue to the Store Manager.
- Repeated damage involving the same product should be reviewed by the Inventory Controller.

**Expected Outcome:** Prevent damaged products from reaching customers and maintain accurate inventory records.
