**SOP-WFM-008: Overloaded Staff Management**

**Purpose:** Define how to identify and manage excessive workloads among store employees.

**Risk Identification**
- More than 10 pending tasks assigned to one employee triggers an overload exception.
- A store is considered overloaded when the average pending task count exceeds 8 per employee.

**Procedure**
1. Monitor pending tasks assigned to each employee.
2. When an employee has more than 10 pending tasks, raise an overload exception.
3. Review task distribution across the store.
4. Reassign pending tasks to available employees with fewer than 5 pending tasks.
5. Record the reassignment and update the task ownership in the system.

**Escalation**
- If all employees are overloaded, notify the Store Manager for further action.
- Repeated workload imbalances should be reviewed to improve staffing and task distribution.

**Expected Outcome:** Balanced workloads, improved operational efficiency, and reduced fulfilment delays.
