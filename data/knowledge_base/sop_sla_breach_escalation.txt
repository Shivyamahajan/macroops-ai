**SOP-SLA-004: SLA Breach Escalation**

**Purpose:** Define how operations teams identify, monitor, and escalate orders at risk of missing their delivery SLA.

**Risk Identification**
- High risk: Fewer than 10 minutes remain before the delivery SLA deadline.
- An order should be reviewed immediately when it is identified as high risk.

**Procedure**
1. The Operations Manager checks the order's current picking and delivery assignment status.
2. Identify the likely cause of delay, such as slow picking, missing items, or rider unavailability.
3. Notify Customer Support when customer communication may be required.
4. For an individual at-risk order, notify the Shift Supervisor and coordinate corrective action.
5. Record the reason for the delay and the action taken.

**Escalation**
- If more than five orders at the same store are simultaneously at high risk, notify the Operations Manager.
- If a store's SLA breach rate exceeds 15% over one hour, escalate the issue to the Regional Manager.
- All escalations must include the affected order count, store, suspected cause, and action taken.

**Expected Outcome:** Earlier intervention, better coordination, and reduced delivery delays.
