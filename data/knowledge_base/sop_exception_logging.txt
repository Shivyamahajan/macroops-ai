**SOP-EXC-007: Exception Logging and Tracking**

**Purpose:** Establish a consistent process for recording, tracking, and resolving operational exceptions.

**Exception Statuses**
- Open: Exception has been identified but no action has started.
- In Progress: A team member is actively investigating or resolving it.
- Resolved: Corrective action has been completed.
- Escalated: The issue requires attention from a higher authority.

**Procedure**
1. Record every operational exception in the system.
2. Include the exception type, severity, affected order or SKU, store, date, and time.
3. Assign the exception to the responsible team member.
4. Update its status as work progresses.
5. Add a resolution note describing the root cause and corrective action.
6. Verify the issue is resolved before closing the exception.

**Response Time Targets**
- High severity: Initial action within 15 minutes.
- Medium severity: Initial action within 60 minutes.
- Low severity: Handle according to the assigned operational priority.

**Escalation**
- Escalate any exception that exceeds its response-time target without action.
- Record the escalation reason and the person or team notified.

**Expected Outcome:** Consistent exception tracking, clear accountability, and faster issue resolution.
