**MacroOps AI: Operational KPI Definitions**

**Purpose:** Define the key performance indicators used to monitor quick-commerce operations.

**1. SLA Breach Rate**
- Definition: Percentage of orders delivered after their promised SLA deadline.
- Target: Below 5%.

**2. On-Time Delivery Rate**
- Definition: Percentage of orders delivered within the promised SLA.
- Target: Above 95%.

**3. Average Picking Duration**
- Definition: Average time taken by staff to pick and prepare an order.
- Target: Below 10 minutes.

**4. Slow Picking Rate**
- Definition: Percentage of orders with a picking duration exceeding 20 minutes.
- Target: Minimize slow-picking incidents.

**5. Missing Item Rate**
- Definition: Percentage of orders containing one or more unavailable items.
- Target: Below 3%.

**6. Average Rider Assignment Delay**
- Definition: Average time between an order becoming ready for dispatch and a rider being assigned.
- Target: Below 5 minutes.

**7. Inventory Accuracy**
- Definition: Percentage of inventory records that accurately match physical stock.
- Target: Above 95%.

**8. Stockout Count**
- Definition: Number of products with zero available physical stock.
- Target: Zero stockouts.

**9. Workforce Overload**
- Definition: Number of employees with more than 10 pending tasks.
- Target: Minimize overloaded employees.

**Note:** These are proposed operational targets for the MacroOps AI demonstration platform, not verified industry benchmarks.
