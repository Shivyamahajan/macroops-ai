**MacroOps AI: Operational Escalation Matrix**

**Purpose:** Define escalation levels, responsible roles, and reporting procedures for operational issues.

**Escalation Levels**

**Level 1 – Shift Supervisor**
- Handles routine operational issues.
- Coordinates immediate corrective actions.
- Monitors picking delays and missing items.

**Level 2 – Store Manager**
- Handles unresolved store-level issues.
- Reviews major inventory discrepancies.
- Manages workforce overload when task reassignment is insufficient.

**Level 3 – Operations Manager**
- Handles multiple simultaneous SLA risks.
- Reviews recurring operational exceptions.
- Coordinates corrective actions across store teams.

**Level 4 – Regional Manager**
- Handles serious or recurring issues across stores.
- Reviews store-level SLA breach rates exceeding 15% over one hour.
- Addresses persistent rider shortages and operational capacity problems.

**Issue-Specific Escalation**

1. Rider Shortage:
   - Delivery Operations Team → Operations Manager → Regional Manager.

2. Inventory Discrepancy Greater Than 10 Units:
   - Shift Supervisor → Store Manager → Inventory Controller.

3. Customer Complaint About Late Delivery:
   - Customer Support → Shift Supervisor → Operations Manager, depending on severity.

4. Workforce Overload:
   - Shift Supervisor → Store Manager → Operations Manager, if unresolved.

**Escalation Requirements**
Every escalation must include:
- Issue type and severity.
- Affected order, SKU, or store.
- Date and time identified.
- Actions already taken.
- Reason for escalation.
- Responsible person or team.

**Expected Outcome:** Clear accountability, faster decision-making, and consistent handling of operational issues.
