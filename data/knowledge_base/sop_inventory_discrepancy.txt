**SOP-INV-001: Inventory Discrepancy Handling**

**Purpose:** Define the procedure for handling differences between system inventory and physical stock.

**Severity Classification**
- Minor discrepancy: 1–10 units.
- Major discrepancy: More than 10 units.

**Procedure**
1. Record all inventory discrepancies in the system.
2. For a major discrepancy, immediately stop picking for the affected SKU and place it on hold.
3. Notify the Shift Supervisor.
4. Conduct a physical recount within 15 minutes.
5. The Inventory Controller must verify and update the system stock after confirmation.
6. Record the discrepancy, recount result, corrective action, and responsible person.

**Escalation**
- If the negative stock variance exceeds 10 units, escalate to the Store Manager within 30 minutes.
- Do not resume picking until the stock discrepancy has been reviewed and the SKU is cleared.

**Expected Outcome:** Accurate inventory records and prevention of incorrect order fulfilment.
