**SOP-DEL-006: Rider Delay and Shortage Management**

**Purpose:** Define the procedure for handling delays caused by unavailable delivery riders.

**Risk Identification**
- A rider assignment delay exceeding 15 minutes triggers an exception.

**Procedure**
1. The Delivery Operations team checks the availability of delivery riders.
2. Identify idle riders in nearby locations and request an assignment.
3. If no rider is available within 5 minutes, manually assign an available rider where possible.
4. Review rider availability before peak periods:
   - Lunch peak: 12:00 PM–2:00 PM.
   - Evening peak: 6:00 PM–10:00 PM.
5. Record the assignment delay and corrective action.

**Escalation**
- If the average rider assignment delay exceeds 12 minutes for one hour, escalate the issue to the Regional Manager.
- Repeated rider shortages should be reviewed for staffing and capacity planning.

**Expected Outcome:** Faster rider assignment, improved delivery coordination, and reduced order delays.
